# Driver data-coverage audit

Generated by `python -m src.driver_audit` (read-only). Full table: `outputs/tables/driver_eligibility_audit.csv`.

## Reference season: 2025

The data ends in 2025, so the app predicts with 2025 drivers in 2025 cars. Nothing from 2026 is used, and no driver without F1 records is shown with a guessed skill profile.

## Evidence policy

The course models read six driver-skill features: two teammate-network ratings (race and qualifying), three teammate-gap features over the last 10 races, and age. A driver is in the prediction pool only if:
- at least 10 career starts (the gap features are 10-race windows),
- at least 5 starts in 2025 (the form season the app uses),
- all six skill features observed (no imputation of driver ability),
- a 2025 performance profile for their car.

Circuit history is **not** required: the models impute a first visit from the training distribution, exactly as for any real driver's first race at a track.
