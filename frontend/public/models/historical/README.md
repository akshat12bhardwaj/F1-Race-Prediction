## Quality and historical limitations

These downloaded models have smooth shaped bodywork, rims, brake parts and named wheel assemblies, but remain simplified. They are not replacements for premium-quality historical chassis scans. The 1980s and 1990s assets are particularly similar in overall construction and must be described as approximate generic era visuals. The 2000s model adds narrower wheel spacing and tire grooves. No distinct 2010–2017 model was found in this supplier's catalogue. Do not describe a 2000s carryover or halo removal as an exact 2010s car.
