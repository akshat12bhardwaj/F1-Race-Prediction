APEX 3D car assets

All cars use local GLB files. These visual bases and original colour patterns are approximate, not exact constructor chassis or historic liveries. No endorsement by the artists, constructors, Formula One or FIA is implied.

  • 2022-2025: Formula F222 Ultimate by CarlosCG31, original model, CC BY 4.0. Downloaded from the BadCodesGG/interactive-f1 distribution, whose changes group parts, replace textures with a neutral finish, and apply Meshopt compression. APEX changes: runtime orientation/scale normalization, audited inward-surface winding/normal repairs, mirrored-transform winding preservation, geometry batching, original three-colour paint patterns, constructor colours and wheel animation. The original downloaded GLB is retained unchanged. 446,632 bytes, 59,884 triangles.
  • 1980s / 1990s / 2000s: generic turbo flat-bottom (15176), high-nose (15173), narrow-track (15177), by 3D Assets, source pack, CC0 1.0. Supplier discloses AI assistance (Claude Fable 5.1). Downloaded unchanged; APEX normalizes, batches, repaints and animates the wheels at runtime. Simplified approximations, especially similar 1980s/1990s bases; premium historical detail remains unavailable.
  • 2010-2017: no independent licensed base acquired. Uses the 2000s GLB as an explicitly labelled carryover approximation, with no halo. This is a known silhouette gap.
  • 2018-2021: generic halo-equipped racer (18685) by 3D Assets, CC0 1.0; simplified approximation, AI assistance disclosed by supplier. Same runtime modifications.
